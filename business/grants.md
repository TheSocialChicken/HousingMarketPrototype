## 1. MIT R&D Samenwerkingsprojecten AI
> **Source:** [RVO.nl](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mit/rd-samenwerkingsprojecten-ai)

**Amount:** 35% of eligible costs · max **€200,000** (small project) or **€350,000** (large project)
**Total budget available:** €3,550,000
**Deadline: April 7 – May 26, 2026** ⚠️ Open now — closes in 18 days
**Lead applicant:** SMEs collaborating together (minimum 2 SME partners)
**Co-funding:** 65% by applicants

**Eligibility:**
- Generative AI and/or machine learning
- Trustworthy and human-centered AI (explainability is a core requirement — directly matches our product)
- Must align with at least one AIC4NL (AI Coalition for the Netherlands) focus area
- Two or more SMEs must collaborate on development

**Our fit:**
- The startup + Züblin Netherlands (or another SME in construction) as co-applicants
- Explainable, human-centered AI is our core design principle — not a stretch
- Construction procurement as the application domain is clearly within scope

**Action required:** Züblin's procurement contact (via Milan) needs to be qualified as a willing co-applicant within the next few days. Alternatively, identify a second Dutch SME in the construction or procurement space as co-applicant.

**Who leads this:** Christiaan (application strategy) + Milan (technical content) + Züblin contact

## 2. KIEM Arbeidsbesparende AI
> **Source:** [Regieorgaan SIA](https://regieorgaan-sia.nl/financiering/kiem/)

**Amount:** max **€40,000** (project total ~€50,000 incl. co-funding)
**Deadline:** September 15, 2026 (14:00 CEST) — next available round
**Lead applicant:** University of applied sciences (Windesheim leads)
**Partners required:** Minimum 2 praktijkpartners including at least 1 SME
**Co-funding:** 25% of requested subsidy, cash or in-kind
**Duration:** 1-year exploratory research project

**Eligibility:**
- "Arbeidsbesparende AI" = labour-saving AI — specifically for AI that reduces manual work
- Procurement automation directly qualifies: the product replaces hours of manual comparison and documentation work
- Construction company + municipality as praktijkpartners (Züblin and one municipality via Gerard)

**Our fit:** STRONG
- Windesheim as lead applicant — already the institutional anchor
- "Labour-saving AI for construction procurement" is an exact fit for this theme
- Low barrier to apply: small amount but validates the research angle and builds the RAAK-PRO application

**Strategic note:** Apply for KIEM first (September 2026). A completed or running KIEM project significantly strengthens a subsequent RAAK-PRO application. Use KIEM to run the minimum training data threshold experiment and generate the first academic output.

**Who leads this:** Christiaan (Windesheim application) + Nina (research design) + Züblin procurement contact as praktijkpartner

## 3. RAAK-PRO
> **Source:** [NWO / Regieorgaan SIA](https://www.nwo.nl/calls/raak-pro) · [Call for proposals Oct 2024](https://www.nwo.nl/sites/nwo/files/media-files/Call%20for%20proposals%20RAAK-PRO%20oktober%202024.pdf)

**Amount:** max **€700,000**
**Last deadline:** March 3, 2026 (passed) — next round TBC, typically every 2 years
**Lead applicant:** University of applied sciences (Windesheim leads)
**Partners required:** Broad knowledge network — industry partners + SMEs + public organisations
**Co-funding:** Minimum 30% of total project costs by partners
**Duration:** Multi-year (typically 3–4 years)

**Eligibility:**
- Long-term practice-oriented research
- Must build sustainable collaboration between hogeschool and professional practice
- Any topic relevant to professional practice — AI procurement qualifies strongly
- National significance preferred

**Our fit:** STRONGEST of all options
- Windesheim is an eligible lead applicant
- Named industry partners: Züblin (construction), Scania (manufacturing procurement), municipality via Gerard
- Research question (minimum training data threshold + procurement AI validation) is well-defined
- NL–Serbia bilateral angle (Windesheim + Belgrade University) adds European dimension

**Partners to name in application:**
- Windesheim University (lead)
- University of Belgrade / Nina Gluhović (research partner)
- ED. Züblin AG Netherlands (industry partner — procurement insider confirmed)
- Scania Netherlands (industry partner — via Ronald de Boer / Christiaan)
- Municipality TBC (via Gerard's network)

**Strategic note:** The March 2026 round has passed. Begin preparation now for the next round. KIEM in September 2026 generates the preliminary research output needed to strengthen the RAAK-PRO application.

## 4. Horizon Europe — Cluster 4: Digital, Industry and Space
> **Source:** [EU Funding & Tenders Portal](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/)

**Amount:** Typically €2–5M+ per project
**Timeline:** 2026–2027 work programme; rolling calls
**Lead applicant:** Research consortium (minimum 3 partners from 3 different EU member states)
**Co-funding:** Typically 70% EU, 30% partners

**Relevant call areas:**
- AI and data in industrial applications
- Digital transformation of SMEs
- Trustworthy AI (explainability, human oversight)
- Circular built environment / construction digitalisation

**Our fit:** MEDIUM — possible but complex
- NL (Windesheim) + Serbia (Belgrade) + one more EU partner = eligible consortium
- Construction procurement AI aligns with industrial AI and trustworthy AI themes
- Application is complex and competitive; realistic only with a dedicated grant writer or consortium coordinator

**Action:** Monitor the EU portal for specific open calls in the Digital, Industry and Space cluster relevant to AI procurement or construction digitalisation. Do not invest significant effort until a specific matching call is identified.
