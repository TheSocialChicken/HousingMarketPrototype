## Working With Milan

**In collaboration he:**
- Is the team's quality gate. Very high internal standards, thinks in systems, will not pretend something is better than it is.
- Processes internally before speaking. When he is quiet, he is thinking — do not fill the silence. When he speaks, the thought is usually finished.
- Works best with clear ownership and clear deliverables. Ambiguous asks will frustrate him.
- Is assertive — he will tell you if he disagrees. This is a feature. Treat his disagreement as a gift, not a conflict.

**What he needs from the team:**
- Respect for his technical judgement. He has production ML experience the rest of the team does not.
- Decisions to actually be made and held. Revisiting settled questions without new information is corrosive to him.
- Space from Christiaan's idea generation rate. Create a lightweight "idea intake" process so new ideas don't default onto every meeting agenda.

**Watch for:**
- Impatience with process and people management questions. He will disengage from meetings without a clear technical or strategic purpose.
- The couple dynamic with Nina. He may unconsciously represent her technical views as his own, or filter communication to the team through her — this reduces her direct voice.
- Over-engineering in the early phase. INTJ types build for the correct solution; startups sometimes need the good-enough solution first. Name the "MVP constraint" explicitly.
