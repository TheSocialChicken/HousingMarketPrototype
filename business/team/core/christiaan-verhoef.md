## Working With Christiaan

**In collaboration he:**
- Generates energy and momentum - ideas come fast, connections happen fast, and he pulls people into possibility.
- Is a strong synthesiser: holds the vision and translates it into language that works for grant bodies, construction industry people, and tech investors simultaneously.
- Performs best with a clear agenda, but will ignore it productively if the conversation opens something better.
- Processes out loud. His thinking is not finished when he speaks it - treat early ideas as exploration, not commitment.

**What he needs from the team:**
- Someone to catch and track what gets decided in high-energy conversations (that's Kirsten's natural role).
- Honest pushback from Milan when the idea outruns the execution reality. He respects directness more than diplomacy.
- Regular grounding check-ins - the ENFP-T turbulent edge means he can become self-critical when progress feels slow. The team should reflect wins explicitly back to him.

**Watch for:**
- Overcommitting externally before internal alignment. He will promise pilots, workshops, and partnerships at a rate that Kirsten and Milan need to sanity-check.
- Energy drops in execution phases. He is a builder and launcher - sustained administrative work drains him quickly.
- The "shiny new idea" pattern during periods of friction or slow progress. When the main thing feels stuck, he generates alternatives. Name this when it happens.
