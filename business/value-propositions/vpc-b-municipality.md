# Value Proposition Canvas — Segment B
## Municipality / Local Government
> AI-Powered Construction Procurement | Last updated: 2026-05-08

---

## Value Proposition Statement

> We help municipal procurement teams meet their legal documentation and compliance obligations without the administrative burden — by providing a locally deployed AI that structures supplier evaluation, applies consistent criteria including sustainability, and generates a complete, audit-ready procurement record for every decision.

---

## Pitch Narrative (30 seconds)

*"Every procurement decision your team makes has to be defensible to auditors, to management, and sometimes to the city council. Today, building that documentation is manual, slow, and inconsistent. We deploy a local AI — on your own infrastructure — that applies your evaluation criteria consistently to every supplier comparison, flags certification gaps, and produces a complete procurement record automatically. Explainability isn't a feature for us, it's the foundation. And because the model runs locally, your procurement data never goes near a commercial cloud."*

---

## Customer Profile

**Who:** Procurement officers, contract managers, and supply chain advisors at Dutch municipalities procuring for public works, infrastructure maintenance, and construction projects.

**Context:** Subject to the Dutch Aanbestedingswet 2012 and EU procurement directives (Directive 2014/24/EU). Required to document all evaluation criteria and decisions. Under political scrutiny. Increasingly required to include sustainability (MVO), circularity (circular economy), and SROI (Social Return on Investment) criteria. Budget cycles are annual (set September–November for following year).

## Segment-Specific Notes

- **Budget timing:** Dutch municipal budgets are set September–November for the following year. Approach decision-makers in Q2–Q3 to be included in next year's budget.
- **Entry point:** A small municipality (gemeente) with a defined infrastructure or housing programme is easier to approach than a large city with complex procurement governance.
- **Credibility accelerator:** A reference from one municipality (or a VNG association endorsement) can unlock many other municipalities simultaneously.
- **Grant angle:** EU Horizon or NWO funding for public sector AI in procurement is realistic with Windesheim as research partner.

## Qualification Criteria

A municipality is a good fit for a pilot if they meet:
- [ ] Active procurement programme (infrastructure, public works, or housing)
- [ ] Dedicated procurement team (not outsourced entirely)
- [ ] Active sustainability / circularity procurement policy
- [ ] IT department open to on-premise or private cloud deployment
- [ ] Decision-maker accessible (head of procurement, CFO, or digital transformation lead)
- [ ] Willing to engage via a research partnership (Windesheim route) if commercial budget not yet available
