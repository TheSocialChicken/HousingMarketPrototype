## Customer Profile

**Who:** Procurement officers, contract managers, and supply chain advisors at Dutch municipalities procuring for public works, infrastructure maintenance, and construction projects.

**Context:** Subject to the Dutch Aanbestedingswet 2012 and EU Public Procurement Directive 2014/24/EU. Required to document all evaluation criteria and decisions. Under political scrutiny. Increasingly required to include sustainability (MVO), circularity (circular economy), SROI (Social Return on Investment), and - from 2026 - CPR environmental performance indicators. Budget cycles are annual (set September–November for following year). Many teams are small relative to procurement volume.

## Compliance Layer: CPR 2024/3110

A distinct and growing pain deserving its own note:

The EU Construction Products Regulation 2024/3110 entered force on 7 January 2025. Key implications for municipal procurement:

- **Mandatory environmental performance indicators** on all construction products on the EU market - phased requirements from January 2026; priority categories fully compliant by January 2028
- **Accredited lab testing required** for all construction products - municipalities must verify supplier compliance, not just accept certificates
- **Documentation obligation:** Purchasing authorities must be able to demonstrate they procured CPR-compliant products - this is a new audit trail requirement on top of existing procurement law

This means municipal procurement teams will need to track an additional dimension of supplier certification for every construction product purchase. Our certification checker can be updated to include CPR compliance status - removing a manual burden that does not yet have a standard tool solution in the Dutch market.

## Segment-Specific Notes

- **Budget timing:** Dutch municipal budgets are set September–November for the following year. Approach decision-makers in Q2–Q3 to be included in next year's budget.
- **CPR urgency window:** CPR 2024 operational requirements begin January 2026. Municipalities buying construction products will need compliant certification tracking before then. This is a natural opening conversation in Q2–Q3 2026.
- **Entry point:** A small municipality (gemeente) with a defined infrastructure or housing programme is easier to approach than a large city with complex procurement governance.
- **Credibility accelerator:** A reference from one municipality (or a VNG association endorsement) can unlock many other municipalities simultaneously - 342 municipalities all face the same compliance obligations.
- **Grant angle:** KIEM and RAAK-PRO are directly applicable; Windesheim leads; municipality via Gerard's HPE Roundtable network as praktijkpartner.
