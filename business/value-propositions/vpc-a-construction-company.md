## Customer Profile

**Who:** Procurement managers, purchasing departments, project planners, and supplier relationship managers at mid-size construction companies (50–500 employees).

**Context:** Active project pipeline, multiple concurrent material procurement needs, working with a mix of preferred suppliers and spot purchases. Procurement is a core function but often under-resourced. Key knowledge often lives with one or two experienced buyers. Staff shortage is acute: 8 in 10 Dutch construction entrepreneurs report it; 20% cannot carry out all work due to lack of people.

## Qualification Criteria

A construction company is a good fit for a first pilot if they meet:
- [ ] Active project pipeline (not project-based / irregular)
- [ ] Minimum 2 years of procurement history in ERP or structured records (for fine-tuning)
- [ ] 2+ buyers in the procurement team (makes knowledge-sharing gain tangible)
- [ ] Recurring material procurement (not one-off custom builds only)
- [ ] Decision-maker accessible (procurement director, CFO, or operations director)
- [ ] IT environment open to on-premise deployment (server or private cloud)
