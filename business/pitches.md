## Segment A - Construction Company

*Audience: Procurement director, CFO, or operations director at a mid-size construction company.*

---

**BING:**
Your best procurement manager costs you €49,500 a year - just in admin. Not salary. Admin. Eleven hours a week comparing quotes in Excel, chasing certificates by email, and writing up reports nobody asked to write. That's the cost of not having a system.

**What:**
We build a procurement AI that your team actually uses instead of the spreadsheet. You give it a specification - what you need, where, by when - and it gives back a ranked comparison of your suppliers in minutes, with certifications verified, reasoning explained, and the report ready for approval. It runs on your own server. Your data never goes anywhere.

**How:**
We spend two to four weeks integrating with your ERP and procurement history. We use that history to fine-tune a local AI model - so it already knows your suppliers, your preferred materials, your past decisions. From day one it's not a generic tool. It's your institutional knowledge, made searchable. The longer you use it, the smarter it gets.

**Are you sure:**
McKinsey estimates the average large construction project runs 80% over budget, with procurement failures - wrong supplier, missed specification, late delivery - consistently among the primary drivers. [1] Construction is the second-least digitised industry on earth - most mid-size firms still run on spreadsheets. [1] When your best buyer leaves, that knowledge walks out the door. We've seen firms cut a three-hour procurement cycle to ten minutes. That's not a marginal improvement. That's a different way of working.

**Can you do it:**
We're led by a Windesheim supply chain researcher who has been running a local AI lab on this problem for two years, and a production ML engineer who has deployed models at Stedin, ABN AMRO, and bliq. We're not building this in theory. The infrastructure exists. The first pilots are the proof.

## Segment B - Municipality

*Audience: Head of procurement, digital transformation lead, or CFO at a Dutch municipality.*

---

**BING:**
Every procurement decision your team makes has to survive a challenge - from a losing supplier, from an auditor, from the city council. You're building that defence case right now in email threads, shared folders, and whoever remembered to fill in the spreadsheet. And from January 2026, the EU Construction Products Regulation adds a mandatory environmental documentation layer on top of everything you're already managing.

**What:**
We deploy a procurement AI on your own infrastructure - not a cloud service, not an external API - that applies your evaluation criteria consistently to every supplier comparison, checks certifications automatically including the new CPR requirements, and generates the complete procurement record at the moment of decision. Not reconstructed after the fact. Built as you go.

**How:**
We configure the system to your procurement policy - your weighting for price, quality, sustainability, SROI, and CPR environmental indicators. For every procurement you run through it, the AI scores each supplier against those criteria, flags any certification gaps, and produces a report that explains every score with a cited data source. That report is your audit trail. It's also what you show the council.

**Are you sure:**
Eighty percent of organisations lack structured tracking of delivery and compliance data [4] - which means their audit trail is retroactive document chasing. The Aanbestedingswet and EU Directive 2014/24 don't accept "we meant to document it." CPR 2024 is now in force and operational requirements start January 2026 - your next procurement cycle will need to demonstrate CPR compliance at the product level. The tooling for that doesn't yet exist in the Dutch market.

**Can you do it:**
We have Gerard Tunteler - 19 years as HPE's Local Government Lead for the Netherlands, founder of the HPE Roundtable for Municipalities. He already has the relationships across all 342 Dutch municipalities. He's not a cold contact for us. He's a colleague. Windesheim University anchors the research side, which means a KIEM or RAAK-PRO grant can fund the pilot before a commercial contract exists.

---

**BING:**
You're running procurement across dozens of concurrent projects, thousands of supplier relationships, and years of contract history - and your ERP tells you exactly what you bought. It has no idea what you should have bought. There is no system that takes a specification and turns it into a ranked, compliant, documented supplier comparison across your entire portfolio.

**What:**
We build that system. A fine-tuned AI procurement assistant that integrates with your existing ERP, meets your sovereign IT requirements, and gives every procurement team in your organisation the same intelligence - regardless of which project they're on or which buyer happens to be in the seat.

**How:**
We connect to your ERP and extract your procurement history - supplier performance, material categories, contract outcomes. We use that data to fine-tune a local model that already reflects your supplier landscape. It deploys on your infrastructure, within your IT perimeter. Every team searches the same knowledge base. Every comparison report is generated with the same criteria. Every decision is documented the same way.

**Are you sure:**
At your scale, even a 5% efficiency improvement on procurement admin is worth more than the tool costs. More importantly: KPMG found that only 31% of infrastructure projects came within 10% of their original budget. [5] The data consistently points to procurement and contracting as the primary cause. You're not immune to that pattern - but you have more data than anyone to fix it.

**Can you do it:**
Sovereign IT deployment is not an add-on for us - it's the product. We have never built a version that sends your data to a third-party cloud. That's the design. On the research side, we're exploring a Windesheim research partnership route that would let a waterschap or infrastructure body participate as a named research partner - which is a lower-risk entry point than a full commercial procurement contract.

## Segment D - Project Developer

*Audience: Development director or CFO at a residential or commercial real estate developer.*

---

**BING:**
Your project managers are calling the same three suppliers they've always used. They're doing it because those are the numbers in their phone. They have no idea whether there's a better option. And when the project finishes, whatever they learned about those suppliers doesn't go anywhere - the next project starts from zero.

**What:**
We give you a simple AI procurement tool that works with your existing project data and does what a dedicated procurement team would do - surface qualified alternatives, benchmark prices, track supplier reliability across your whole portfolio.

**How:**
You enter a material specification - what you need, what standards it has to meet, when you need it. The tool searches your supplier history and a broader qualified supplier database, returns a ranked list with prices, certifications, and delivery track records, and flags anything that looks risky. Your project managers make a faster, better-informed decision. You keep a record of it. Next project, that record is part of the baseline.

**Are you sure:**
Materials are 40-55% of your total project cost. [6] Price volatility, wrong specifications, and unreliable suppliers are the three most common sources of budget overrun in residential development - and none of them require a procurement disaster to cost you money. They just require a slightly wrong assumption at the order stage, multiplied across a few projects a year. That's your margin.

**Can you do it:**
We offer a lighter hosted tier for this segment - you don't need your own infrastructure, you don't need an ERP, and you don't need a procurement department to run it. It's designed for exactly the team size you have. If you're already a customer of one of our construction company clients, there's a warm referral route that gets you set up in weeks.

## Segment E - Material Supplier

*Audience: Sales director or key account manager at a construction material manufacturer or distributor.*

---

**BING:**
Construction procurement is going AI. Buyers are increasingly using tools that match a specification to a supplier list automatically - they don't search a relationship, they search a database. If your product data isn't in that database, structured and current, you don't appear in the comparison. You don't lose - you're not even considered.

**What:**
We make sure you're in the comparison. We onboard your product catalogue and certifications into the AI matching engine that construction procurement teams use - in a format that matches how buyers now search, not just how suppliers have always presented.

**How:**
We work with your sales or marketing team to structure your product data - specifications, certifications, environmental performance indicators, availability - into a machine-readable format. That data sits in the matching engine. When a buyer specifies a material that matches your products, you appear. When your certifications are current, you pass the filter. When your delivery track record is strong, that's a scored criterion too.

**Are you sure:**
The shift is already happening. Our buyer clients - construction companies and municipalities - are moving their procurement to AI-assisted tools precisely because the manual process is too slow and too error-prone. The suppliers who benefit from that shift are the ones who are in the system. The ones who aren't are the ones who keep relying on relationships that are slowly being bypassed by the tool their customer is now using.

**Can you do it:**
We're starting with a supplier data partnership - no cost, mutual benefit. You get visibility; we get better data for our matching engine. Every supplier we onboard makes the matching more accurate for all buyers. As the buyer base grows, we'll introduce a paid supplier tier with analytics showing you exactly how often you're appearing in comparisons and why you're being selected or passed over.

## Auditors / Certification Bodies

*Audience: Business development or partnerships lead at Kiwa, TÜV Rheinland, Lloyd's Register, or SGS.*

---

**BING:**
Every AI procurement tool in construction is trying to verify supplier certifications in real time. Right now, they're doing it by emailing your clients and waiting. You're the authoritative source of that data - and you're not in the loop.

**What:**
We want a data partnership. Your certification database feeds directly into the AI procurement systems that construction companies and municipalities use. Instead of a buyer chasing a certificate manually, the system checks with you at the moment of comparison. Current or expired. Valid or flagged. Instantly.

**How:**
Through an API or a licensed data feed, your certification status - for materials, processes, or sustainability claims - becomes a live field in every supplier comparison. When a buyer queries a supplier, the system checks your database. If the certificate is current, the supplier passes. If it's expired or missing, the buyer is flagged before the order goes anywhere.

**Are you sure:**
CPR 2024 is now in force. From January 2026, municipalities and construction companies procuring in the EU need to demonstrate product-level CPR compliance - accredited lab testing, performance indicators, documented at the point of purchase. The demand for automated, real-time certificate verification is about to spike significantly. You're the credible source. We're the integration point.

**Can you do it:**
We handle the integration. You define the terms of the data licensing. What you get: market visibility with every buyer client we onboard, reduced inbound manual verification requests from procurement teams, and a formal position in the compliance layer of AI-driven construction procurement. The conversation we need to have is about the data licensing model - not whether this makes sense.

## Trucking / Logistics Companies

*Audience: Business development or data partnerships lead at Van den Bosch, Vos Logistics, or a comparable construction materials logistics provider.*

---

**BING:**
Delivery reliability is the third most common cause of construction project delays - after wrong specification and wrong supplier. Construction procurement teams know this. They just don't have the data to score it before they place an order.

**What:**
We want your delivery performance data. On-time rates, lead time variance, damage rates - by supplier and material category. That data becomes a live scoring criterion in the AI procurement tool construction teams use to select suppliers.

**How:**
Through a data feed or API from your TMS, we pull anonymised delivery performance metrics - aggregated per supplier, not per shipment. When a construction buyer compares two concrete suppliers, they see price, certifications, and delivery reliability as scored dimensions side by side. The supplier with the better delivery track record gets ranked higher. That's a direct line between your operational data and the buyer's decision.

**Are you sure:**
Materials account for 40% of construction project cost, and late delivery is a direct cause of project schedule overruns - which cost 10–15% margin on affected variations. Construction procurement teams desperately want to factor delivery reliability into supplier selection. They just don't have access to systematic delivery data. You do.

**Can you do it:**
We're proposing a data partnership - your delivery data enriches our matching engine; we provide attribution to your logistics services in the supplier comparison output. For you, it's a visibility play: construction companies see your network's reliability data in every comparison, which is a credible sales argument when buyers are selecting logistics alongside materials. We'd scope this as a V1 feature integration if the data quality supports it.

## CO₂ / Sustainability Bodies

*Audience: Partnerships or innovation lead at DGBC, RVO, SBK/Nationale Milieudatabase, or a comparable sustainability body.*

---

**BING:**
Construction procurement is where sustainability commitments either get enforced or quietly abandoned. Municipalities have MVO mandates. Construction companies have CSRD Scope 3 obligations. The CPR 2024 is now law. But at the moment the buyer sits down to compare suppliers and place an order, the environmental performance data isn't in the room. It's in a PDF on a website somewhere.

**What:**
We want to put it in the room. We're building an AI procurement assistant for construction teams, and we want to integrate the Nationale Milieudatabase as a live data source for environmental performance scoring. Every time a buyer compares suppliers, CO₂ impact and environmental performance are scored alongside price and certification - not as an optional extra, but as a standard criterion.

**How:**
Through an API or licensing arrangement with SBK, the NMD's environmental performance data - EPDs, climate performance scores, material category benchmarks - feeds into the specification matching engine. When a buyer specifies a structural concrete or an insulation panel, they see which suppliers meet the environmental performance threshold alongside the price and certification criteria. The comparison report that goes to the approval layer includes the environmental scoring with cited NMD data.

**Are you sure:**
CPR 2024 makes environmental performance indicators mandatory on all construction products sold in the EU - accredited data, phased requirements from January 2026. Municipalities are legally required to include circularity and sustainability criteria in public procurement under the Aanbestedingswet. CSRD forces large construction companies to document Scope 3 supply chain emissions from 2025 onwards. The compliance stack is in place. The data source exists. The missing link is the integration into the procurement decision at the moment it's made.

**Can you do it:**
The integration is our responsibility. What we're asking for is a data licensing conversation and, potentially, a credibility endorsement - if the environmental scoring in our tool is powered by NMD data, that's a significant validation for both the tool and the database. From your perspective, it gets NMD data into the hands of the people making purchasing decisions - which is where it has to be to change behaviour.
