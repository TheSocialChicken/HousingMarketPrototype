# AI-Powered Construction Procurement

A locally deployed, fine-tuned AI assistant that helps construction procurement teams find materials, compare suppliers, and generate audit-ready reports — without their data ever leaving their own infrastructure.

---

## The Problem

Construction procurement teams waste hours every week digging through emails, Excel sheets, and supplier portals just to compare a handful of quotes. Certification tracking is manual. Supplier knowledge lives in one person's head. And when a decision gets questioned, the paper trail barely exists.

For municipalities and public bodies, the problem is compounded by legal compliance requirements — every supplier evaluation must be documented, consistent, and defensible to auditors and elected officials.

---

## The Solution

A fine-tuned AI procurement assistant, deployed on the client's own infrastructure, that:

- **Matches materials to specifications** — translate a procurement need into a ranked list of qualifying suppliers
- **Compares suppliers automatically** — price, delivery time, quality, reliability, certifications, and sustainability in one report
- **Generates audit-ready documentation** — every recommendation includes a full reasoning trail, suitable for approval workflows and legal review
- **Learns from the client's own data** — the model is fine-tuned on their procurement history, making it more accurate over time and creating a meaningful switching cost
- **Runs locally** — no data sent to external APIs; full data sovereignty for privacy-sensitive and public sector buyers

## Target Customers

| Segment | Why it fits |
